Trapped is a short film that follows a girl who tries to escape a labyrinth-like site. However, every turn, open door, or form of communication sends her right back where she started. Nothing is at seems. Words make no sense, pathways which were an alleged mistake keep her trapped. The reason for her escape or her backstory are unknown. But that is perhaps what makes it all the shackling: the fear of the unknown. The film is meant to represent the human mind, how certain setbacks can sometimes be both in and out of our control. You can control your actions, the path you take, your journey. But ultimately the destination and result ae not in our control. If you notice, the girl in the film is constantly circling around the same space and panics when she realizes she is at the same destination. The film invites audience to act and detach from the fear of the unknown.
